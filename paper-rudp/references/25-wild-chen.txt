Abstract (OpenAlex record of the IEEE publication, retrieved 2026-11-25), excerpt:
... we propose a simple yet effective learning-based wireless loss diagnosis (WiLD) solution for enhancing wireless congestion control. WiLD uses a neural network (NN) to accurately distinguish between wireless packet loss and congestion packet loss. To seamlessly cooperate with rule-based CCAs and make real-time decisions, we further implement WiLD in Linux kernel to avoid the frequent kernel-space communication. Specifically, we use a lightweight NN for inference and propose an integer quantization for WiLD deployment in various Linux versions. Real-world experiments and simulations demonstrate that WiLD can accurately differentiate the wireless and congestion packet loss with negligible CPU overhead (around 1% of WiLD vs. around 100% of learning-based algorithms such as Vivace and Aurora) and fast inference time (45% less compared to TensorFlow Lite).
