Verification and availability:
Edition 02/2019 is listed as in force; it covers the configuration, fault and performance management of ADSL and VDSL2 systems (G.992.x and G.993.2). Free from ITU-T, but the ITU web server rejected automated requests; download it from the link in a browser.

Used for: the performance counters and line parameters used by the line manager (ES, SES, FEC corrections, full initializations, SNR margin, ATTNDR, NDR, LATN) in 15-minute and 24-hour intervals (Sections 1, 2.1 and 5.1).
